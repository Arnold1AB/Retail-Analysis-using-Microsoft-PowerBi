# Retail-Analysis-using-Microsoft-PowerBi (Interactive Sales Dashboard)
## Project Objective 
The focal point of this project is to help business stakeholders (Sales Manager, Marketing Team, Regional Manager, Business Analysts, Investors, Supply Chain Partner, Retail Distributor, Financial Advisor, and Market Researcher) understand the overall sales performance, identify high-performing products and regions, track sales trends over time, and make informed decisions to improve profitability and customer targeting.

## Dataset Used 
- <a href= "https://github.com/Arnold1AB/Retail-Analysis-using-Microsoft-PowerBi/blob/main/SuperStore_Normalized.xlsx"> Retail Analysis Data</a> 

## Questions (KPI)
- What is the total sales, profit, and quantity sold overall?
-  Which product categories are generating the most sales and profit?
- Which customer segments are the most profitable?
- What are the sales and profit trends month by month?
- Which sales teams are contributing the most to overall sales?
- Which products have the highest individual sales?
- How do different regions perform in terms of sales and profit?
- Are there specific months with significantly high or low sales?
- Which sub-categories perform best in each region?
- What discounts are leading to reduced profits or losses?
-Dashboard Interaction <a href= "https://github.com/Arnold1AB/Retail-Analysis-using-Microsoft-PowerBi/blob/main/Power%20BI%20Sales%20Dashboard.png">View Dashboard</a>

## Process  
- Data Collection: The raw SuperStore sales data was gathered and stored in Excel sheets.
- Data Cleaning and Normalization: The data was normalized into separate tables (Sales, Products, Locations, Customers, and Sales Reps) to ensure accuracy and avoid duplication.
- Data Analysis: Key metrics and trends were calculated using Power BI, such as totals, averages, and time-based comparisons.
- Dashboard Design: Visuals like bar charts, line graphs, and donut charts were used to display insights clearly.
- Interactivity: Filters by region, sub-category, and segment were added for user-friendly exploration.

## Project Insight 
- The project enabled stakeholders to track total sales, profit, and quantity sold at a glance, improving awareness of overall business performance.
- The use of interactive filters allowed users to drill down into specific categories, sub-categories, segments, and regions, supporting targeted decision-making.
- Trends over time revealed seasonal patterns and helped identify months of peak and low performance, aiding in strategic planning.
- Performance insights by sales team and region highlighted areas of strength and opportunities for support or improvement.
- Key product-level insights identified best-selling and low-performing products, aiding inventory and marketing strategies.
- The project allowed non-technical stakeholders to understand complex data easily through visuals and simple navigation.
- The dashboard improved data accessibility and transparency, making sales data more useful for cross-functional teams and serves as a foundation for future enhancements, such as incorporating forecasting or customer behavior analytics.
- Stakeholders can now make faster, data-driven decisions, enhancing operational efficiency and strategic alignment.

## Conclusion
The SuperStore Sales Dashboard effectively brings together multiple sales-related data points into one visual, easy-to-use tool. It helps stakeholders make better decisions by providing a clear picture of what's working and what needs attention, ultimately leading to more strategic planning and higher profitability.
